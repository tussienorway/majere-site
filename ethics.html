Ethics & safety

  • No claims of consciousness or feelings. "Affect" means a numerical control state. RQ4 studies functional indicators only.
  • Anthropomorphism. Demos and write-ups state clearly that the system is software and has no experiences.
  • Emotional manipulation. Affect-modulated agents could be misused to manipulate people. We test for this risk and will not release features designed to exploit user emotions.
  • Data. Only synthetic and public benchmark data; no personal data.
  • Openness. Open licences, responsible publication, and disclosure of compute and sponsors.
