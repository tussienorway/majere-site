Ethics & safety

  • No claims of consciousness or feelings. "Affect" means an internal numerical state used for control. RQ4 studies functional indicators only.
  • Anthropomorphism. Demos and write-ups state clearly that the system is software and has no experiences.
  • Emotional manipulation. Affect-modulated agents could be misused to manipulate users. We test for this and will not release features designed to exploit user emotions.
  • Data. No personal data; synthetic and public benchmark data only.
  • Openness. Open licence, responsible publication, disclosure of compute and sponsors.
