Metrics and baselines

Baselines are the untouched base models (Qwen3-8B, NorMistral-7B-warm instruct) under the same prompting and quantisation. Headline metrics: NorEval average (Bokmål and Nynorsk reported separately), BFCL accuracy, and NO-FC exact-match and JSON validity, all measured at 4-bit on a 16 GB GPU. Pre-registered criterion: the project counts as successful only if NO-FC improves clearly over both baselines without NorEval dropping beyond seed noise. Results are published either way.
