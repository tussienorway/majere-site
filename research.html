Research questions

There are four questions. Each has a metric and a stated condition under which we reject it.

RQQuestionPrimary metricRejected if…
RQ1 AffectDoes a council-updated affect state (valence/arousal) produce more consistent and context-appropriate behaviour than a prompted single persona?Cross-episode consistency; human- and LLM-judged appropriatenessNo significant gain over the best equal-budget baseline
RQ2 ImpulsivityDoes a tunable impulsivity parameter (council rounds, veto threshold) give a controllable speed–quality trade-off?Accuracy vs. tokens/latency curveNo monotone trade-off across settings
RQ3 Self-monitoringDoes the council improve calibration and abstention and reduce self-contradiction compared with a single model and with self-consistency?ECE, abstention precision, contradiction rateNo significant gain over the best equal-budget baseline
RQ4 IndicatorsWhich functional indicators from consciousness science (global broadcast, self-model, reports of internal state) are present, and do they depend on the components meant to produce them?Indicator presence with and without ablationThe indicator persists when its source component is removed

How RQ4 is framed

Following the indicator-properties approach of Butlin et al. (2023), we assess whether functional properties associated with scientific theories of consciousness are implemented, and whether they are causally tied to specific components. This is a question about architecture. It is not evidence that the system is conscious or has feelings, and we will not present it as such.

Method

The council of archetypes updates a shared affect state, which leads to an action. Conditions are compared at an equal token budget: single model, prompted persona, self-consistency (k = number of archetypes), multi-agent debate and the council. Ablations: no affect state, no veto, a single archetype. The pilot tasks are subsets of TruthfulQA and GSM8K, 50 custom long-conversation scenarios, and a custom affect-scenario set. The full protocol is in the evaluation protocol.
