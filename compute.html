Compute ask

The pilot runs on our own single 16 GB GPU. Grant compute is for the scaled phase (M2–M3). These are planning estimates, not measurements. The pilot will replace the assumed throughput with measured numbers, and the request will be updated.

Estimation method

Training FLOPs ≈ 6 × N × T (N = 8×109 parameters, T = training tokens). This is the standard dense-transformer approximation, and it is conservative for LoRA. GPU-hours = FLOPs ÷ (peak bf16 throughput × utilisation). We assume an H100 at about 989 TFLOP/s dense bf16 and 35% utilisation, which gives about 3.5×1014 FLOP/s effective.

The same work on our single 16 GB card would take months, and some runs (longer sequences, multiple seeds in parallel) don't fit at all. That's why the scaled phase needs grant compute. On TPUs the equivalent is a v4/v5e slice for a few weeks.
