Compute request

Summary: ≈400 A100-80GB GPU-hours (or a TPU v4-8 / v5e slice for 30–60 days) over 9 weeks to fine-tune and distil a 9B open model for Norwegian and reliable tool/computer use, released as open GGUF files that run on a 16 GB consumer GPU. Applicant: Thomas Austenaa, independent researcher, Grimstad, Norway (no institutional affiliation).

Measured on our own GPU (2026-10-13): QLoRA smoke run, Qwen3-8B 4-bit, rank 16, max length 1024, 50 steps on an RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB: peak VRAM 9.41 GiB, 15.3 s/step, ~327 training tokens/s. That is ~1.2M tokens/hour: 10M tokens ≈ 8.5 h, 100M tokens ≈ 85 h, and a single seed of a 300M-token mix ≈ 11 days. Three seeds plus ablations are not feasible on one card, which is the reason for the request. The estimate above will be rescaled from these numbers once the full pilot finishes.

Evidence so far

  • Technical preprint (4 pages): PDF · Markdown
  • Open evaluation set + harness: huggingface.co/datasets/tussienorway/majere-eval-no-tools
  • Baseline (measured, RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB): Qwen3.5-9B 98.3% Norwegian, 81.7% right tool, 70.0% exact arguments.
  • Negative result (measured): LoRA-finetuned Qwen3.5-4B 90.0% / 63.3% / 53.3%. Local 4B training does not reach the 9B baseline; 9B training with seeds and ablations does not fit our GPU.
  • Model cards: model-cards/ · Public demo: HF Space · Code: GitHub
