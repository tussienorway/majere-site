Compute needs

Why Majere needs GPU access, and how it will be used.

Why

Council deliberation multiplies model calls per task, and every comparison must be repeated against equal-budget baselines and several ablations. To test whether findings hold beyond small models, the full study must run on larger open models (30–70B), which need data-centre GPUs or TPUs.

Plan

  1. Pilot – one open 7–8B model, locally or on free Hugging Face ZeroGPU, small task subsets.
  2. Full evaluation – larger open models, full task sets, all baselines and ablations, multiple seeds.
  3. Public demo – a Hugging Face Space showing the council deliberating.

Programs we are applying to

  • Lambda Research Grant
  • Hugging Face Community GPU Grant (for the public demo)

In return: open code, open results (including negative results), and a disclosure of how much compute was used and who provided it.
