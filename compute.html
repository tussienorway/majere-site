Compute needs

Why

Council deliberation multiplies model calls per task, and every result must be repeated against equal-budget baselines, ablations and several seeds. Testing whether the effects hold beyond small models requires 30–70B open models, which need data-centre GPUs or TPUs.

Phases

  1. Pilot. One open 7–8B model, run locally or on free Hugging Face ZeroGPU.
  2. Full evaluation. Larger open models, full task sets, all conditions and seeds.
  3. Public demo. A Hugging Face Space showing the council's deliberation.
