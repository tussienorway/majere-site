Related work

How Majere relates to existing theory and methods. Every reference below is a published work. arXiv identifiers were checked against the arXiv API.

Positioning

Multi-agent debate and self-consistency already show that multiple samples or agents can improve reasoning. Majere asks a narrower question: does adding a shared affect state and a controllable deliberation depth help beyond these methods when the budget is the same? It also borrows the indicator-properties method to test which consciousness-science indicators are architecturally present, without claiming consciousness.
